Connect accounts (Google)

Lets each person connect their own Google account on their page, so the assistant can read their Gmail, Calendar and Drive for them (read only). Free: you make your own Google “OAuth client” once.

How to get the Client ID and secret (about 10 minutes)
  1. On a computer, open console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Google account. Make a new project (top left → New project → name it “Message Assistant”).
  2. Menu → APIs & Services → Library. Turn on (Enable): Gmail API, Google Calendar API, Google Drive API.
  3. Menu → APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform). App name: Message Assistant. User type: External. Your email as support and contact email. Save.
  4. On the consent screen, Audience: either add each person’s Gmail under Test users (up to 100; Google then asks them to connect again every 7 days), or press Publish app (no 7-day limit; people see a “Google hasn’t verified this app” page once and tap Advanced → Continue).
  5. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID. Type: Web application. Under Authorized redirect URIs add exactly: …
  6. Press Create. Copy the Client ID and the Client secret into the boxes below and press Save.
