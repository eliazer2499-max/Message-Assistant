Use your own Google keys (free, about 5 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com, make a project, and enable the Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive APIs.
  2. Set up the OAuth consent screen (External, your email as support/contact).
  3. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Web application. Under Authorized redirect URIs add exactly: …
  4. Paste the Client ID and Client secret here and press Save, then tap Connect Google.
